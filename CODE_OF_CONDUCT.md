# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Be honest about what you know. Don't let anyone get hurt.

## Behaviour we expect

- **Assume good faith.** People come to this project with wildly different backgrounds — some
  have run fiber lasers for years, some just unboxed their first machine.
- **Be specific rather than dismissive.** "That won't work" helps nobody; "that won't work
  because Split Marking is galvo-class and this is a GRBL device" helps everybody.
- **Grade your claims honestly.** Overstating what you know is the one thing here that can
  actually injure someone. An honest "I don't know" is always welcome.
- **Report near misses.** If something fired or moved unexpectedly, say so. Nobody will give you
  a hard time about it, and the next person will be safer.

## Behaviour we won't accept

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments.
- Pressuring anyone into a test they are not comfortable running. Everything in this project is
  voluntary, and some of it involves a 100 W laser.
- Presenting guesses as confirmed findings, or restoring unverified M-codes into shipped profiles
  after they have been removed. This is a safety rule, not a style preference.
- Publishing WeCreat's code, firmware, or profiles here. We document findings, not their
  property.

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone is representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers via a private issue or direct contact. Reports will be handled
discreetly. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions, and may block accounts, for
behaviour that violates this document.

Safety violations — restoring unverified codes, pressuring others into unsafe tests — will be
acted on immediately and without a warning cycle.
