Subject: Lumos Ultra - GRBL status report returns Idle during job execution (LightBurn streaming)


Hello,

I own a Lumos Ultra (100W MOPA) and I have been setting it up with LightBurn,
which the Ultra product page lists as supported software. I believe I have
found a firmware issue, and I wanted to report it properly rather than just
complain in a forum. I would be glad to be told I have got this wrong.


THE OBSERVATION

Over the USB serial connection, the controller's GRBL status report appears to
return a fixed state. Every "?" query answers:

    <Idle|MPos:2.748,18.237,0.000|FS:0,0|Pn:Z|WCO:0.000,0.000,0.000>

  - MPos is correct and live. It tracks real position and updates during
    motion.

  - The state field is always "Idle", including while a job is actively
    running.

  - FS is always "0,0" rather than reporting actual feed and speed.

The clearest case: if I send "!" (feed hold) in the middle of a job, the
controller still answers "Idle". A machine that has just been commanded to
hold reports itself as idle.


WHY IT MATTERS

LightBurn uses that state to pace how fast it sends commands. Because the
machine always reports itself idle and never busy, long jobs stall part-way
through. There is no alarm and no disconnection - the fan keeps running and
engraving simply stops. Pressing Pause and then Resume restarts it.

MakeIt is unaffected, and I think I understand why. MakeIt uploads the job to
the machine's SD card over the USB network interface and lets the controller
run it locally, so it never streams over the serial port and never depends on
this. That is a perfectly reasonable design - it just means the serial
acknowledgement path may not have had the same exercise.


WHAT I HAVE RULED OUT

Cable and port (direct connection, several ports), LightBurn transfer modes,
DTR, position-query polling, the laser-fire button, and the Z-axis settings
that fix the "Busy after framing" problem on other WeCreat machines. None of
them changed the behaviour.

MakeIt runs flawlessly on the same cable, which is what pointed me at the
streaming path rather than the hardware.


MY QUESTION

Is the GRBL status report on Lumos-family firmware intended to report "Run"
and "Hold" states, and if so, is this a known issue on the Ultra?

I would also be grateful to know whether an official LightBurn configuration
for the Lumos Ultra is planned. I have built an unofficial one and would much
rather point people at yours.


ONE SMALLER THING THAT MAY BE USEFUL

The Ultra identifies itself over serial as:

    [WeCreat Lumos :ver 000240]

There is nothing in that string distinguishing it from a Lumos. That means
your official Lumos LightBurn profile connects to an Ultra without any warning
and applies a 116 x 116 mm workspace instead of 210 x 210 mm. Owners could
easily ruin work without understanding why. A version or model string that
differed would prevent that entirely.

I have documented everything publicly, with the raw captures, in case it is
useful to your engineers:

out of it.
