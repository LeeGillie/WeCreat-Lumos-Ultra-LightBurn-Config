3. THE MODEL IDENTIFICATION STRING

This was in my first email and I do not think it has been answered yet.
I think it is the one issue in the whole set that can currently damage
someone's work.

The Ultra identifies itself over serial as:

    [WeCreat Lumos :ver 000240]

Nothing in that string distinguishes an Ultra from a Lumos. Your
official Lumos LightBurn profile therefore connects to an Ultra with no
warning at all and applies a 116 x 116 mm workspace to a 210 x 210 mm
machine. The job is silently wrong, and the owner has no way to know
why.

A distinct model or version string would prevent this completely, and it
would cost nothing at the point of manufacture.

Is that something engineering would consider?
