UPDATE: both WeCreat and LightBurn answered - within a day - and the
Lumos Ultra / LightBurn problem now has an owner and a fix in progress.

A few days ago I posted about jobs stalling and drifting when driving a
Lumos Ultra from LightBurn, and published everything I had measured. I
want to follow that up, because the story has changed and it would be
unfair to leave the gloomy version standing.

WHAT HAPPENED - AND WHO ACTUALLY DID IT

I want to name people, because "the vendor responded" undersells what
happened here.

ALLEN, WeCreat Customer Support.

Replied the next morning to a long, technical, unsolicited bug report
from a customer he had never heard of. He confirmed almost all of the
findings, and told me WeCreat is already working with LightBurn on the
right fix - uploading the job to the machine's memory and letting the
controller run it, rather than streaming it line by line over USB. That
is how MakeIt has always worked, and it is why MakeIt has never had this
problem. Test builds are close.

Then he did something I genuinely did not expect. I had questioned a
published specification - the UV spot size on the Ultra product page.
Rather than defend it or route me into a queue, he checked it, agreed it
was wrong, explained exactly how the error had crept in, and said
WeCreat will correct their materials. A manufacturer correcting its own
spec sheet because one customer asked a careful question is rare, and it
should be said out loud.

He has also taken personal ownership of following the issues through.
Please do not start asking for him by name in your own tickets - the
point is that WeCreat support handled this properly, not that there is
one good guy to hunt down. Let the man work.

AARON (Aaron.F), LightBurn Support Team.

Went straight to the mechanism rather than the symptom, explained
precisely how LightBurn decides its transmit buffer size and why this
machine confuses it - and then said a fix for the related bug was
already written. No deflection, no "reinstall and see".

JON C (goeland86), LightBurn Dev Team.

Came into the thread with hardware in hand and told me something I could
not have found on my own: that on this controller a large buffer causes
moves to be dropped outright. He is in direct contact with WeCreat's
engineers, has been candid about what LightBurn does and does not yet
know, and offered to go and check his own machine's firmware version for
me. That is a developer treating an owner's bug report as worth his
afternoon.

Two companies, one day, no defensiveness from either. Credit where it is
due.

The whole exchange is public and worth reading, if only as an example of
how this is supposed to go:

https://forum.lightburnsoftware.com/t/wecreat-lumos-ultra-controller-status-report-never-leaves-idle-streams-desynchronise-on-long-jobs/192109

WHERE THIS ACTUALLY LEAVES OWNERS TODAY

Being honest, because that is the whole point of the project:

- Vector work from LightBurn: works, and the geometry is confirmed
  correct - 210 x 210 mm, correct placement, no measurable field
  distortion at 200 mm.
- Large fills: still not trustworthy yet. That is the part waiting on
  the upload path.
- If you own an Ultra, still do NOT import WeCreat's official Lumos
  profile. The Ultra reports itself over USB as a plain "Lumos", so that
  profile will connect without any warning and apply a 116 x 116 mm
  workspace to a 210 x 210 mm machine. Your job comes out silently
  wrong.
- The Frame button can fire the beam on these machines. It is not a
  gantry laser and the old habits do not transfer. Eye protection on.

WHAT WE FOUND THIS WEEK

It turns out MakeIt writes the complete job it generates onto your own
PC before sending it, so WeCreat's own output can be studied without
touching the machine at all. One real emboss job came to 222 MB and
11.3 million lines - which, at the speed of the USB serial link, would
take about 39 minutes just to transmit. That single number explains why
uploading is the right answer and streaming was never going to be.

The more useful find: within that one job, the fiber laser's frequency
and pulse width change 282 times. So the machine happily accepts MOPA
parameter changes mid-job. That matters, because it means proper MOPA
control from LightBurn - colour on stainless, controlled heat on brass -
is a real prospect rather than wishful thinking. Testing that is next.

TWO THINGS YOU CAN HELP WITH

1. Your firmware version. Connect over USB and note what the machine
   reports on connect. Mine says [WeCreat Lumos :ver 000240]. A LightBurn
   developer has seen an Ultra behave better than mine and suspects a
   newer build exists, so version numbers from real owners are genuinely
   valuable right now.

2. Other models, and PLEASE include your settings. If you run a Flex,
   Lumos, Vision or Vista with LightBurn, I would love to know whether a
   dense fill completes for you - but "it works fine for me" on its own
   unfortunately does not help.

   What makes a report usable is three numbers:

     - the SPEED you ran at (mine fail at 12000 mm/min)
     - the FILL TYPE - plain Fill, or Offset Fill
     - which SOURCE - diode, fiber, MOPA, UV

   The reason is that the fault looks like the machine running out of
   buffered commands faster than USB can refill it. A slow job never gets
   the chance to starve, so it sails through and tells us nothing. A fast
   one is the real test.

   Chris Zambesi has already run the first one - a 90 x 90 mm fill on a
   Flex with the diode, which completed cleanly. That is genuinely
   encouraging, and I have asked him for the speed so we can tell whether
   it was a fair fight or simply a gentle one.
