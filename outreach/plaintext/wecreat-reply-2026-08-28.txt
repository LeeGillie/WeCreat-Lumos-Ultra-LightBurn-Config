3. THE MOST USEFUL THING I HAVE FOR YOUR ENGINEERS

This came from LightBurn's public forum since your last message, so
there is nothing confidential in it - but I do not think it has been put
in front of your firmware team as a pair of numbers yet.

    LightBurn's Lumos Ultra ... ver 000236 ... fills run clean
    My Lumos Ultra ........... ver 000240 ... fills stall and drift

The working machine is on the OLDER firmware, by four increments. Their
engineer's own word for it was "regression".

And the obvious objection does not hold. I expected their clean result
came from running gently, but the opposite is true - they were marking
at 1500 to 2000 mm/s, while my failures happen at 200 mm/s. They were
running seven to ten times faster than me and had no trouble.

That matters because it is backwards. If this were a communication
buffer running empty, the FAST machine should fail and the slow one
should be fine. It is the other way round.

I want to be careful: their machine was on the UV source and mine is the
100W MOPA, so it is not a perfectly matched comparison, and I would not
call this proven. But if something changed between 000236 and 000240
that affects this, that is a far more tractable problem than the
architectural one we have all been assuming - and you are the only
people who can see the changelog between those two builds.
